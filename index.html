Plan your dynasty roster

Enter your Sleeper username (or paste a league ID) and load your league. The desk reads every roster and rookie pick in the league and prices them with FantasyCalc's dynasty superflex values.

  1. My team grades each position against your league mates as a Strength, Average or Need, and flags aging rooms.
  2. League is a heat map of every team, so you can see who has surplus where.
  3. Trade ideas pairs your surplus with each manager's surplus and keeps deals inside your fairness band.
  4. Calculator lets you build and test any deal by hand.

Mode changes how players are weighed. Contend leans on this season's production. Rebuild discounts players past their position's age cliff and favours picks and young players.

Values: FantasyCalc dynasty values, matched to your league's team count, superflex and PPR settings. League data: Sleeper public API. Nothing you enter leaves your browser except requests to those two services.
